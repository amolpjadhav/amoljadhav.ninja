# The Haunted Arcade: A Seven-Stop Treasure Hunt

Yesterday a parcel arrived with no return address: a treasure hunter's journal, and on its last page, a hand-drawn map of the arcade on 5th street — dark for years — with seven stops circled in red. *"Every legend starts with a single clue,"* the final entry reads. *"Follow my trail through all seven stops, and the lost high score is yours."* This is a treasure hunt, not a quiz: every room opens only when the one before it is solved, wrong answers never punish, and your progress survives a closed tab.

## Tips for little hunters

- **Say patterns out loud.** In the ghost-lights room, naming each light as it blinks (“red, blue, blue…”) doubles how many you can hold. Memory loves a voice.
- **Chunk the lights.** Don’t remember six blinks — remember two groups of three. Your brain files boxes faster than lists.
- **Turn pictures into numbers.** If 3 apples make 12, one apple is 4 — then the banana gives itself away.
- **Wrong answers teach.** Every wrong tap tells you something. The hunt only moves forward; it never takes progress away.
