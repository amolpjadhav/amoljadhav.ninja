# Newton's Laws of Motion: Push It, Race It, Bounce It

Over 300 years ago, a man named Isaac Newton watched the world move — falling apples, rolling carts — and wrote down three rules for how everything pushes and gets pushed. (The Moon's circling needs one more of his ideas, gravity — that's another article.) Every car, football, rocket, and skateboard still obeys them today.
